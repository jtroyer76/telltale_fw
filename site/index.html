Telltale

Firmware for a touch display that shows your boat's Signal K data. For the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-7. Version ….

This browser can't install firmware. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https to install.

Connect the display to this computer by USB, click Install, and choose its serial port. Erase the device on a first install. To update a display you've set up, don't erase: its WiFi and screens are kept.

After installing

  1. The display shows Set up WiFi with two QR codes.
  2. Scan the first with your phone's camera to join the display's own WiFi, Telltale-XXXX.
  3. The setup page usually opens by itself. If not, scan the second code, or open http://192.168.4.1.
  4. Choose the boat's WiFi network and save. The display restarts, joins it, and looks for the Signal K server on the network.

Later: the gear at the top right of the display opens Settings, with the display's address, the device password, and Change WiFi. The setup page is also at http://telltale.local/ on the boat network.
